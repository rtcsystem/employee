Help & FAQ

How do I book a room?

Click Book a Room in the sidebar. You'll be taken to the Booking System already signed in — no need to log in again.

How do I update my profile or photo?

Click your name/avatar in the sidebar to open My Profile. You can update your info and change your photo from there.

How do I change my password?

Open My Profile → Change Password tab. This works for both Employee and Admin accounts.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login screen, click Forgot password?. You'll get a 6-digit OTP by email to set a new password.

Where can I find company documents?

Use the Folders section in the sidebar to browse and download shared company files.

How do I post an update on the Feed?

Posting is limited to Admins, to keep the Feed focused on company announcements. Employees can view and comment on posts.
